Why this work

Khipus

The Inka recorded administration in knots. Structure is the data. That is why this is ML on the Open Khipu Repository, not another chatbot.

Quechua

Most tokenizers assume spaces and European morphology. Southern Quechua does not. If NLP is going to work in the Andes, the tokenizer has to.

LATAM
